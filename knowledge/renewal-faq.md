# Owl Shoes — Renewal & Billing FAQ

## When does my subscription renew?

Owl Shoes memberships renew automatically on the same calendar day you
originally signed up, every 12 months. You will receive a reminder email
30 days before renewal and a text reminder 7 days before.

## How much does renewal cost?

The standard Owl Shoes membership renews at $89 per year. Premium
memberships renew at $149 per year. Pricing is locked for your first two
renewal cycles — your price will not increase before then.

## What happens if my payment method is expired?

If we cannot charge your payment method, your membership enters a 14-day
grace period. During the grace period you keep all benefits. If payment
still fails after 14 days, the membership is paused until payment
information is updated. No late fees are ever charged.

## How do I update my payment information?

Update your card any time at owlshoes.example/account, or ask our phone
agent to text you a secure update link. Updating your card takes effect
immediately and does not change your renewal date.

## Can I cancel instead of renewing?

Yes. Cancel any time before your renewal date and you will not be charged.
You keep member benefits until the end of the paid period. Cancellations
within 30 days after an accidental renewal receive a full refund.

## Do you offer refunds?

Renewal charges are fully refundable within 30 days if you have not placed
a member-discounted order in that window. Refunds are returned to the
original payment method within 5 business days.

## What are member benefits?

Members get free 2-day shipping, 15% off all full-price shoes, early access
to seasonal drops, and free returns for 90 days.

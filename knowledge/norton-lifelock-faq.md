Question: When does my Norton LifeLock membership renew? Answer: Your Norton
LifeLock membership renews automatically at the end of your current term. That
is one year from your enrollment date on an annual plan, or the same day each
month on a monthly plan. Your exact renewal date is shown in your account under
Billing, and we email a reminder before each renewal charge.

Question: How do I update my payment method or change my card? Answer: To update
your payment method, sign in to your account, go to Billing, and select Update
payment method. You can use a credit card, a debit card, or PayPal. The change
applies to your next scheduled charge. If your renewal is within twenty-four
hours, update it right away so your protection is not interrupted.

Question: What happens if my payment fails or my card is declined? Answer: If a
renewal payment is declined, we retry the charge over the next several days and
email you each time. Your membership stays active during that grace period. If
the payment still cannot be collected after the retry window ends, your
membership lapses and identity monitoring stops until you update your payment
details.

Question: Will I lose my identity protection if my membership lapses? Answer:
While a membership is lapsed, monitoring and alerts stop and restoration support
is not available for new incidents. Your case history is kept, so if you
reactivate within sixty days your previous alerts and monitored items are
restored automatically.

Question: How do I turn off automatic renewal or cancel my membership? Answer:
To stop automatic renewal, go to Billing and select Manage automatic renewal,
then switch it off. Your membership stays active through the end of the term you
already paid for and simply does not renew after that. Automatic renewal is on
by default because any gap in coverage leaves gaps in identity monitoring.

Question: Can I get a refund on my Norton LifeLock membership? Answer: Annual
memberships can be refunded in full within sixty days of the initial purchase or
of a renewal charge. Monthly memberships can be refunded within fourteen days of
the most recent charge. Refunds go back to the original payment method and
usually appear within five to ten business days.

Question: Why was I charged a different amount than last year? Answer: Your
introductory price applies to the first term only. At renewal the membership
moves to the standard rate for your plan, and tax is calculated from your
billing address, so the total can differ from what you first paid. The renewal
reminder email sent before each charge shows the exact amount.

Question: Can I change or upgrade my plan? Answer: You can upgrade at any time,
and the price difference is prorated against whatever remains of your current
term. A downgrade takes effect at your next renewal date so you keep the
coverage you already paid for. Plan changes are made under Membership in your
account.

Question: What payment methods do you accept? Answer: We accept major credit and
debit cards including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover, and
PayPal in supported regions. Prepaid cards cannot be used for an automatically
renewing membership because they cannot be reliably charged at renewal time.

Question: How do I find my past invoices or billing history? Answer: Every charge
appears under Billing, in Order history. Each entry has a downloadable invoice
showing your plan, the term dates, the amount, and tax. Invoices stay available
for seven years.
